# Playlist navigation

When tabs overflow the available width, use the left/right arrows or the mouse wheel over the tabs. The horizontal scrollbar is hidden. The selected tab is brought into view after selection and window resizing; scrolling through tabs does not change the selected playlist. The down-arrow button lists all playlists in a bounded, scrollable menu with a mark beside the selected playlist. Names are shown literally, including underscores, and have accessible labels and full-name tooltips.

Click an album heading to select its visible matching tracks. Drag the heading to move the contiguous album block shown beneath it, preserving song order and occurrence identities. Drop in the upper/lower half of a target heading or song row to place the block before/after that target album. The accent insertion line shows the boundary. Hold the pointer near the top/bottom of the song list to scroll while dragging. Right-click the heading, or focus it and press Shift+F10, for Move album up/down without dragging.

The heading's existing folder/album identity is retained: same-named albums from different folders/discs are distinct. Separated occurrences of the same album are not gathered automatically by dragging; only the dragged contiguous block moves. Album movement is unavailable while search or import is active, or album headings are disabled. Stale, foreign-window and other-playlist payloads are refused. Self-drops preserve the order. Music and CUE files remain unchanged; the new playlist order is saved to the existing SQLite store. Playback and queue identities are preserved.
